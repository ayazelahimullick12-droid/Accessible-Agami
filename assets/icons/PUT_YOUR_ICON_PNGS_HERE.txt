The app's icons live here as SVG drawings (loan.svg, savings.svg, home.svg ...).
They are flat pictures of things from village life: a hand with bangles
receiving taka, a clay money bank, a tin-roof house, a woman in a saree,
a passbook, a kolshi full of coins.

To use your own picture for one of them: save it here as a PNG with the same
name (for example savings.png) and delete the .svg. If neither file exists,
the app shows the emoji written in the page instead.

The Android build and the Render site pick up this folder automatically.
